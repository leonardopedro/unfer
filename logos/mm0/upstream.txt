# Statements rewritten from MM0 theorems that already exist, into the CNL of
# `logos mm0`.
#
# Source: MM0's own Peano-arithmetic development, `examples/peano.mm0`
# (statements and axioms) and `examples/peano.mm1` (proofs), from
# https://github.com/digama0/mm0 (the checkout read while writing this corpus was
# at `/tmp/mm0/`; the licence is CC0-1.0, recorded in `../../ATTRIBUTION.md`).
# Every entry quotes the upstream name and statement, so the CNL line can be
# checked against the source by eye:
#
#     theorem add12: $ a + 1 = suc a $ = '(eqtr addS (suceq add0));
#
# `suc` is written either as `suc a` or as `the successor of a`; both are the
# same term, so they produce the same certificate under the same name.
#
# Why a second corpus at all: `claims.txt` was written to fit the fragment.
# These sentences were written for a real development first and squeezed into
# the fragment second, which is the only way to find out where the fragment and
# the theory stop — and the expectations below are the honest answer, not what
# would make the run green:
#
#   * `proved:`   the recursion laws (`add_z`/`add_s`/`mul_z`/`mul_s`) settle it
#   * `unproved:` true, but upstream proves it by *induction* (`peano5`), which
#                 this theory does not have; the pipeline must say so, not guess
#   * `true:`/`false:`  ground instances of an upstream theorem, decided by
#                 evaluation on one side and refutation on the other

# ── the recursion axioms, as the theorems upstream derives from them ────────
# add02: $ a + 0 = a $
proved: for all a: a plus zero equals a
# mul02: $ a * 0 = 0 $
proved: for all a: a times zero equals zero
# addS2: $ a + suc b = suc (a + b) $
# The parentheses are load-bearing in the CNL too: `the successor of a plus b`
# is `(suc a) + b`, exactly as `suc a + b` is in the MM0 source.
proved: for all a, b: a plus the successor of b equals the successor of (a plus b)
# mulS2: $ a * suc b = a * b + a $
proved: for all a, b: a times the successor of b equals a times b plus a
# add12: $ a + 1 = suc a $
proved: for all a: a plus one equals suc a
# add12, in the other spelling: the same claim, the same certificate.
proved: for all a: a plus one equals the successor of a
# add22: $ a + 2 = suc (suc a) $
proved: for all a: a plus two equals suc (suc a)

# ── consequences that need congruence, not induction ────────────────────────
# The `eqeqd addeq2`/`muleq2` steps of the upstream proofs, stated as claims:
# a zero in an inner operand is rewritten inside the outer operator.
proved: for all a, b: (a plus zero) plus (b plus zero) equals a plus b
proved: for all a: a plus (zero plus zero) equals a
proved: for all a: (a plus zero) plus zero equals a
proved: for all a, b: a times (b times zero) equals zero
proved: for all a, b: (a times b) times zero equals a times zero
# add12 read right-to-left: `suc a` is `a + 1`.
proved: for all a: suc a equals a plus one
# add22 one level out, and with the successor on the left: the recursion
# consumes the *right* operand, so this is a different path through the rules.
proved: for all a: suc a plus two equals suc (suc (suc a))
# mulS2 at a successor operand: `a * suc a = a * a + a`.
proved: for all a: a times suc a equals a times a plus a
# the `suceq add02` step of an upstream proof, stated as its own claim:
# congruence under `suc` wrapping a rewriting step.
proved: for all a: suc (a plus zero) equals suc a
# both operands' zero stripped before the product (eq_mul_l / eq_mul_r over
# add_z twice) — the `muleq2` shape with a variable on each side.
proved: for all a, b: (a plus zero) times (b plus zero) equals a times b
# congruence under `times` on the left alone: `eq_mul_l` carries the add_z
# premise as an argument, but the rewriter never needs it — it normalises the
# operand in place, which is why the premise-carrying rules are not in RULES.
proved: for all a, b: (a plus zero) times b equals a times b

# ── the induction-only group, honestly unproved ─────────────────────────────
# Each of these is *true in the standard model* and unreachable in the
# equational fragment: `teq` has a model where `tz + a` is stuck, so no
# reflexivity/congruence/recursion chain can prove them. `unproved` here is the
# correct answer, and the reason the corpus records rather than hides.
unproved: for all a: zero plus a equals a
unproved: for all a: one plus a equals suc a
# `two + a` is addS1 twice over add01 — the same induction boundary, one more
# shape: rewriting gets stuck on the variable right operand either way.
unproved: for all a: two plus a equals suc (suc a)
unproved: for all a, b: suc a plus b equals suc (a plus b)
unproved: for all a, b: suc a plus b equals a plus suc b
unproved: for all a, b: a plus b equals b plus a
unproved: for all a, b, c: (a plus b) plus c equals a plus (b plus c)
unproved: for all a, b, c: a plus (b plus c) equals b plus (a plus c)
unproved: for all a, b, c: (a plus b) plus c equals (a plus c) plus b
unproved: for all a, b, c, d: (a plus b) plus (c plus d) equals (a plus c) plus (b plus d)
unproved: for all a: zero times a equals zero
unproved: for all a: a times one equals a
unproved: for all a: one times a equals a
unproved: for all a: a times two equals a plus a
unproved: for all a: two times a equals a plus a
unproved: for all a, b: suc a times b equals a times b plus b
unproved: for all a, b: a times b equals b times a
unproved: for all a, b, c: a times (b plus c) equals a times b plus a times c
unproved: for all a, b, c: (a plus b) times c equals a times c plus b times c
unproved: for all a, b, c: (a times b) times c equals a times (b times c)
unproved: for all a, b, c: a times (b times c) equals b times (a times c)
unproved: for all a, b, c: (a times b) times c equals (a times c) times b

# ── false, and the fragment says so by declining ────────────────────────────
# `a + 1 = a` is false in the standard model, but refuting a *universal* claim
# would need a counterexample search, which this fragment does not have. It says
# `unproved` — not `false` — and that distinction is the point of keeping the
# line: no verdict is honest, a guessed "no" would not be.
unproved: for all a: a plus one equals a
unproved: for all a: suc a equals a plus two

# ── ground instances: one numeral assignment per upstream theorem ───────────
# addcom at 3, 4
true: 3 + 4 = 4 + 3
# addass at 1, 2, 3
true: (1 + 2) + 3 = 1 + (2 + 3)
# add4 at 1, 2, 3, 4
true: (1 + 2) + (3 + 4) = (1 + 3) + (2 + 4)
# add01 at 9
true: 0 + 9 = 9
# add11 at 4
true: 1 + 4 = suc 4
# addSass at 2, 3
true: suc 2 + 3 = 2 + suc 3
# mulcom at 3, 4
true: 3 * 4 = 4 * 3
# muladd at 2, 3, 4
true: 2 * (3 + 4) = 2 * 3 + 2 * 4
# addmul at 2, 3, 4
true: (2 + 3) * 4 = 2 * 4 + 3 * 4
# mulass at 2, 3, 4
true: (2 * 3) * 4 = 2 * (3 * 4)
# mul11 at 5
true: 1 * 5 = 5
# mul12 at 7
true: 7 * 1 = 7
# mul21 at 3
true: 2 * 3 = 3 + 3
# mul22 at 3
true: 3 * 2 = 3 + 3
# mulS1 at 3, 4
true: suc 3 * 4 = 3 * 4 + 4
# mul01 at 5
true: 0 * 5 = 0
# add4 again, with larger numerals
true: (5 + 6) + (7 + 8) = (5 + 7) + (6 + 8)
# addass at 4, 5, 6
true: (4 + 5) + 6 = 4 + (5 + 6)
# mulS1 at 7, 8: the successor sits on the *left* operand, so the recursion
# still runs on the right one — `suc 7 * 8` is `(suc 7) * 8`, as in the source.
true: suc 7 * 8 = 7 * 8 + 8
# muladd written in number words
true: three times (four plus five) equals three times four plus three times five

# ── misquotations of the same theorems, which must be refuted ────────────────
# addcom with the second operand misconverted: 7 = 8 is false.
false: 3 + 4 = 5 + 3
# add12 with the successor off by one: 5 = 6 is false.
false: 4 + 1 = suc 5
# mul22 with the right side misconverted: 6 = 5 is false.
false: 3 * 2 = 3 + 2
# mul01 wrong: 0 = 5 is false.
false: 0 * 5 = 5
# muladd missing the second distributive term: 14 = 10 is false.
false: 2 * (3 + 4) = 2 * 3 + 4

# ── the same sentences asked as questions ───────────────────────────────────
true: is 3 + 4 equal to 4 + 3?
false: is 2 * (3 + 4) equal to 2 * 3 + 4?
proved: for all a: is a plus zero equal to a?

# ── names that collide with the theory's own constants ──────────────────────
# `tz` is the theory's zero. A CNL variable called `tz` must become `v_tz`, or
# the emitted binder would capture the constant.
proved: for all tz: tz plus zero equals tz
proved: for all ts: ts times zero equals zero
